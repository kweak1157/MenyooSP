## Where

- `Solution/source/Scripting/GTAentity.cpp` `RequestControl` (~621)
- `RequestControlOnce` (~614)
- Timeout overload (~632) still uses `WAIT`

## Problem

No-arg `RequestControl` retries `RequestControlOnce` 12 times in the same frame. Comment says do not `WAIT`. The game cannot give control in that loop. The timeout overload `WAIT`s and stops the trainer.

## Fix

One try per frame, or a timer across frames. Do not busy-loop. Do not `WAIT` on the menu thread for control.

## Do not

- Do not break Spooner delete/attach that needs control.
- If a caller must wait, make that caller use a later frame, not a same-frame loop.
